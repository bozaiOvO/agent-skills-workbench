# Livestream Analysis Rubric

Use this rubric to review a livestream transcript. Score each dimension from 1 to 5 and focus on the lowest-leverage bottlenecks first.

## Rule Of Thumb

If `人群/定位/产品/价格/信任` is weak, do not spend all the effort polishing wording. Fix upstream logic first, then rewrite lines.

## Dimensions

### 1. Strategic Fit

Look for:
- is the target audience clear
- is the offer understandable
- is the room goal explicit

Common failure:
- speaking broadly to everyone
- giving content with no conversion path
- price or offer feels disconnected from the room

High-leverage fix:
- define one audience, one room goal, one main conversion action

### 2. Persona And Trust

Look for:
- does the speaker sound like a believable operator, guide, or peer
- is there a compact human story or authority anchor
- are proof points concrete

Common failure:
- all expertise, no person
- all emotion, no credibility
- generic self-intro with no trust signal

High-leverage fix:
- add a short persona story, one proof point, and one value stance

### 3. Opening And Hook

Look for:
- is there a reason to stay in the first minute
- does the opening match the room goal
- is the promise clear

Common failure:
- long warm-up with no hook
- self-intro before relevance
- vague topic title

High-leverage fix:
- open with the audience problem, the promised outcome, and why this speaker can help

### 4. Structure And Rhythm

Look for:
- clear beginning, middle, and conversion path
- topic order feels intentional
- segments do not drag

Common failure:
- random topic hopping
- too much repetition without escalation
- no transition from content to action

High-leverage fix:
- simplify into opening plus 3 main segments plus close

### 5. Content Clarity

Look for:
- one point at a time
- examples and definitions are easy to follow
- audience can repeat the idea after hearing it once

Common failure:
- abstract talk with no example
- too many concepts stacked together
- expert language that loses the viewer

High-leverage fix:
- shorten each idea, add one example, and end each segment with one takeaway

### 6. Interaction And Live Feel

Look for:
- reactions to comments, questions, or guest interactions
- viewer participation has a purpose
- the room feels alive rather than pre-recorded

Common failure:
- pure monologue
- interaction with no strategic function
- weak handling of audience questions

High-leverage fix:
- design specific interaction checkpoints and tie them to trust or conversion

### 7. Offer And CTA

Look for:
- clear next step
- repeated but not chaotic invitation
- reason to act now

Common failure:
- content-heavy room with almost no ask
- aggressive CTA with weak trust
- multiple CTAs competing with each other

High-leverage fix:
- keep one primary CTA, explain who it is for, and bridge from content to action

### 8. Repurposing And Topic Supply

Look for:
- usable clips, stories, opinions, and examples
- links between short-video topics and the livestream
- repeatable content engine

Common failure:
- useful room but nothing easy to clip
- no stable topic source
- persona material is present but never packaged

High-leverage fix:
- extract value statements, case slices, and strong opinions into separate content angles

## Default Output Format

Use this compact format when the user wants a standard review:

1. Overall verdict
2. Top 3 blockers
3. Scorecard
4. Rewrites
5. Next-stream checklist
