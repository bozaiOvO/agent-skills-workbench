# Course Syllabus

This file maps all 21 transcripts into a usable course structure. Use it when you need to know which file likely contains the relevant method.

## Day 1: Business Foundation, Monetization, Sales, And Student Diagnosis

### `001.第一天上午1.txt`

- course opening and positioning
- why personal IP sells `人设` first
- why every public-facing teacher needs a usable persona story
- authority anchors, credibility, and emotional trust setup

### `002.陪跑案例展示.txt`

- showcase of client outcomes and coaching cases
- examples of how positioning, products, private-domain conversion, and livestream review fit together
- useful for seeing how the methodology is packaged into offers

### `003.第一天下午1.txt`

- monetization and operating system
- why doing livestream is not the goal; making money is
- insistence on an end-to-end system from persona to livestream to conversion to delivery

### `004.第一天下午2.txt`

- conversion and operations
- `筛选` over brute-force selling
- conversion as a system rather than a trick
- relationship, persona, and process as the real driver behind `成交`

### `005.第一天学员指导1.txt`

- student diagnosis on legal-services IP
- useful for how the course diagnoses positioning, monetization difficulty, and high-ticket fit
- shows how a narrow positioning can still fail if the offer chain is weak

### `006.第一天学员指导2.txt`

- student diagnosis around finance and other businesses
- product ladder, front-end/back-end, margins, and pricing
- useful for debugging weak product structures behind weak livestream conversion

### `010.第二天下午1.txt`

### `011.第二天下午2学员互动.txt`
