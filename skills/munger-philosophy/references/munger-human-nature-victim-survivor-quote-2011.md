# Munger human nature / victim-survivor quote — 2011 interview

Use when the user asks whether a Chinese short-video quote like “我不会因为人性而感到失败/意外” is really Charlie Munger.

## Reliable wording found

The short-video wording “我不会因为人性而感到失败” is likely a misquote or mistranscription. The source wording aligns with “意外 / surprised,” not “失败 / failed.”

English passage found via Right Attitudes article quoting a 2011 CNN interview with Poppy Harlow about David Sokol’s conduct during Berkshire Hathaway’s Lubrizol purchase:

> “It’s not my nature … when you get little surprises as a result of human nature … to spend much time feeling betrayed. I always want to put my head down and adjust. I don’t allow myself to spend much time ever with any feelings of betrayal. If some flickering idea like that came to me, I’d get rid of it quickly. I don’t like any feeling of being victimized. I think that’s a counterproductive way to think as a human being. I am not a victim. I am a survivor.”

## Background

- Interview: 2011 CNN interview with Poppy Harlow.
- Context: David Sokol, then seen as a possible Buffett successor, violated company standards around Berkshire Hathaway’s Lubrizol purchase and left.
- Question: whether Munger felt betrayed.
- Meaning: Munger said he felt sad, but did not spend much time feeling betrayed; he adjusts rather than inhabiting victimhood.
